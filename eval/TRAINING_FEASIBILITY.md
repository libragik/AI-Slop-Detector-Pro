# Training our own video detector: feasibility and blockers

Reviewed September 4, 2026 local time. This is a feasibility assessment, not a training experiment. It used existing manifests, provenance/license assessments, completed development results, official public metadata, and local hardware availability checks. No calibration predictions, new media, model weights, classifier calls, training steps, accounts, or terms acceptance were involved. Application code and frozen studies are unchanged.

**Training a compact video detector is technically plausible on this machine. Training a trustworthy replacement from the data currently assembled is not yet justified.** A new training objective could address a real mismatch: distinguish generatively synthesized pixels from conventional CGI and edited camera footage, while detecting short generated intervals. The present corpus does not adequately supervise or independently test those distinctions. More fitting on its consumed examples would demonstrate adaptation to those examples, not that the Instagram failure is solved.

The recommended next investment is a bounded training-data and supervision audit, beginning with the publicly available GenBuster-200K-mini training release, alongside a plan for independently documented camera, CGI, and generated families. The subsequent [archive-index audit](./TRAINING_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) confirmed 9,800 binary-labeled video entries but no source-family or CGI/mixed sidecars. Do not launch a large training run or promote another fitted score before those data gates pass.

## What the failures justify

The frozen [shot/window study](./AEGIS_SHOT_DEVELOPMENT.md) falsely flagged 19 of 45 negative files, including unedited conventional CGI and camera-origin timelapse. All 102 windows completed without errors or unsupported intervals. Better sampling therefore did not establish the correct origin distinction. These are five parents connected through donor edits, not 45 independent sources. The four planned generated parents and 60 positive derivatives were stopped and **do not exist as acquired training data**.

Earlier [development evidence](./RESEARCH_DECISION.md) also separates several problems: severe repost simulations damage specialist score ranking; the frozen application missed a directly observed generated insert; an earlier centered-window specialist missed that insert despite sampling its generated interval. Conversely, the old 0.5- and 1-second benchmark inserts were not sampled by the eight-frame Corvi/SPAI recipes, so those outcomes do not prove a failure to recognize their pixels. A training hypothesis must address representation, supervision, sampling and aggregation together; changing a threshold alone does not resolve all four.

These failures support investigating a different learned objective. They do not prove that a particular architecture or more training will generalize to unseen generators and Instagram delivery.

## Supervision missing for the intended product

| Missing distinction | Required records and design |
| --- | --- |
| Conventional CGI/VFX versus generative video | Many independently authored render scenes, engines, materials, motion and postproduction styles, with source projects and render settings; retain CGI as an explicit negative category. Two scenes cannot define this boundary. Include genuine camera edits, timelapse, stabilization and composites so “unusual motion” or an edit boundary is not a surrogate AI label. |
| New generators and source independence | Generator/version/job receipts, prompt and reference-asset families, and original output hashes. For camera footage: owner permission/rights, capture/session/device records and preserved source files. Split complete ancestry families; reserve entire generator/version families prospectively. A file hash or a generator marketing label alone is insufficient. |
| Short or spatially partial generation | Verified generated parents and non-generative parents, exact edit intervals and frame lineage, including inserts away from fixed sample positions, transitions and spatial masks. A fully generated clip label cannot tell a temporal model which short interval should trigger a result. Blend weights describe construction before compression, not the probability that a decoded pixel is generated. |
| Instagram delivery | Authorized matched originals and their actual platform downloads, post IDs, retrieval receipts, codec/dimension/cadence records and repeated versions grouped together. Balance delivery conditions across labels. Existing mild/severe encodes test our recipes, not Instagram's complete pipeline. |
| Audio and modality boundaries | Keep the first training objective visual-only unless a separate, adequately sourced human/synthetic audio corpus and evaluation are established. Silent benchmark clips and one narration pair do not support an audio verdict. |

Sampling labels and origins must remain outside model inputs. Training must prevent shortcuts from archive layout, source watermarks, captions, dimensions or label-specific encoders. Matched transforms, source-aware splits and explicit quality-only controls can expose some shortcuts, but do not manufacture missing provenance.

## Hardware and a credible training scope

Live checks found an **Apple M3 Ultra, 256 GiB physical memory and 32 logical CPU cores**. The isolated `eval/.venv` has PyTorch 2.8.0 with MPS built and available; CUDA is unavailable. PyTorch documents MPS support for training on Mac GPUs. This establishes a plausible execution route, not compatibility or throughput for a particular video architecture. [PyTorch 2.8 MPS documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.8/notes/mps.html).

The completed shot candidate required about 2.14 GB peak process RSS and 147.6 seconds for 45 files/102 windows on CPU. Those are inference measurements. Training needs saved activations, gradients and optimizer state, and may use different kernels; neither those timings nor unified-memory capacity establishes training time. The BusterX project's default eight-H100 training configuration is a different CUDA-oriented MLLM workflow, not a demonstrated Mac recipe or a requirement for every compact detector. [Official BusterX training instructions](https://github.com/l8cv/BusterX).

A defensible research hypothesis is a licensed compact visual backbone with a temporal objective that distinguishes camera, conventional rendering and generation, supervised on verified intervals and evaluated with a fixed whole-clip aggregation rule. Limited fine-tuning is more plausible here than training a foundation video encoder from scratch. A frozen-feature head can be a fair low-cost baseline, but cannot recover evidence discarded by sampling or prove that an inadequate representation separates CGI from generation. No backbone has been selected or cleared for this new training scope.

Before a training run, a small forward/backward contract must verify the chosen operators and preprocessing, finite gradients, actual parameter updates, CPU/MPS agreement, peak memory, checkpoint save/resume, and bounded decode/training throughput. Estimate full-run time only from that measurement. Do not silently port CUDA-specific kernels, use unsafe checkpoint loading, or treat successful inference as a training contract.

## Decision gates

1. **Establish a usable dataset.** Audit a bounded training-release subset and source records; reject or quarantine unclear rights/labels. Create a separate training allocation. Build sufficient independently documented camera, conventional CGI and generated ancestry families to cover the missing distinctions before fitting a product candidate.
2. **Declare one learning hypothesis and its control.** Freeze modality, sampling, temporal labels, augmentation balance, family splits, loss, aggregation and a short candidate budget. Tune only in training/development families; count clip false positives after aggregation, including increasing clip duration and window count. Do not optimize each quality stratum against its test outcomes.
3. **Preserve a real test.** Collect and freeze a previously unexamined set with source/session and generator/reference-family separation, actual platform pairs and verified short inserts. Reuse existing consumed failures as development challenges only. Report per-category recall, false-positive rate, abstentions/errors, localization/coverage and latency, with ancestry-aware uncertainty. Correlated versions cannot multiply the source denominator.
4. **Require independent improvement before integration.** Compare the frozen candidate with a frozen control on that set at a declared false-positive operating point. Fit any probability calibration only after model selection. Until that gate passes, model scores remain uncalibrated evidence and the product cannot claim reliable detection from training success alone.

The immediate blocker is a trustworthy, diverse and separable training/evaluation corpus with the right labels. This Mac can support a bounded compact-model experiment once that is addressed; it cannot compensate for missing supervision or turn a repeatedly consumed benchmark into independent evidence.
