# Decision after the failed DINOv2 experiment

Checked 2026-09-05 UTC. **No additional local model run is justified now.** This bounded check found one scientifically different, small residual detector, NPR, but did not establish a compatible commercial-use license for its weights or a validated video inference contract. Defer it; do not substitute another threshold, head, or aggregation rule into a failed experiment.

The completed DINOv2 candidate falsely flagged 42/45 documented camera/CGI negatives and 6/36 author-real quality cases. AI detections were 17/23, 16/23 and 17/23 across original/mild/severe processing. Complete coverage and zero processing errors rule out missing-window failures as an explanation. There was no net temporal benefit. The positive mixed control does not establish insert recognition: its maximum occurred in a CGI-only window, and the clean CGI control was also positive. [Preserved experiment](./DINOV2_TRAINING_DEVELOPMENT.md).

## The one new route checked: NPR

[NPR's primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.10461v2) studies local neighboring-pixel relationships left by generator upsampling. It subtracts a nearest-neighbor downsample/upsample reconstruction from the input before a small learned CNN. This targets local residual structure rather than DINOv2 semantic embeddings. The paper uses ProGAN/LSUN training and older still-image GAN/diffusion evaluations. It supplies neither a temporal video detector nor evidence establishing conventional-CGI specificity, current video-generator transfer, or actual Instagram recompression performance. Its published image accuracy is not our measured video accuracy.

## What would change the decision

NPR is a defensible residual-feature hypothesis, but “smooth pixels,” absent camera noise, or resampling correlations are not sufficient origin labels. Those properties can change under conventional rendering and compression. The published learned classifier cannot be replaced by a handcrafted cutoff with an implied reliability claim. Training another head on the same weak-label archive would still lack documented CGI, camera-family and mixed-origin supervision; it would require its own prospective study and improved source evidence, not another pass over consumed diagnostics.

A local NPR screen would first require explicit weight rights, one pinned official checkpoint/preprocessing choice, restricted safe loading and a CPU numerical contract. It would then need a predeclared camera/CGI negative gate and matched whole-generated/mixed/recompression challenges. Those prerequisites are currently unmet. No new local experiment is proposed for immediate execution.

The practical next access-dependent route is the separately prepared bounded specialist API check. The user has no specialist account; no account, purchase or upload occurred here. An account would enable a measurement, not establish service quality. Any candidate surviving development would still need [fresh source-grounded validation](./INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION_READINESS.md), a separate frozen decision, and actual Instagram round-trip evidence. The readiness document's pass condition was not met by DINOv2, so its proposed media acquisition/generation remains unexecuted. Consumed diagnostics and the old holdout cannot become independent confirmation.

This audit read primary source/code/metadata only. It downloaded no model or media, ran no inference/training, accepted no terms, and changed no detector behavior.
