# Accuracy-claim audit

Checked 2026-09-04. This is a limitations audit of the evaluation, not a detector scorecard.

After the documented final freeze, the 75-clip holdout was unsealed and the [final comparison](./HELDOUT_RESULTS.md) did not establish an accuracy gain. The final scorer's audio-only amendment was based on development controls before unsealing; its replay changed zero score fields or decisions on the saved holdout observations. These clips are now consumed as a holdout. They must not be used to select a new policy and then presented again as an untouched test of that policy.

## What the corpus establishes

The 230 original clips have 230 distinct SHA-256 hashes and 230 registered file-identity groups. For all 230, the archive filename stem used to construct `groupId` equals the downloaded file's SHA-256. No registered group or exact file hash crosses development, calibration, and holdout in the supplied manifests; this does not establish semantic-source, creator, capture-session, or prompt independence. Actual allocation is development 125 (61 generated / 64 author-labeled real), calibration 30 (15 / 15), and holdout 75 (39 / 36). The initial 35 smoke clips are permanently development. All 690 original/transformed rows collapse to the same 230 registered groups. See [CORPUS_SOURCE_LIMITS.md](./CORPUS_SOURCE_LIMITS.md) for the verified archive inventory and stronger source requirements.

The six constructed controls contain only two underlying scenarios: three excerpts from the same Sintel film and three insert durations using the same real/generated parent pair. They can expose conventional-CGI confusion or short-insert misses. They cannot establish population accuracy on CGI or mixed videos. Keep the control group and all its parent groups in development.

## Limits on claims

1. **Ground truth is author asserted.** GenBuster per-clip camera provenance, creator IDs, generation prompts, and original creation histories are missing. Our harness copies the authors' labels rather than inferring them with a model, but undocumented mislabels, related creators/prompts, and training contamination cannot be excluded. The verified hash-derived grouping detects registered byte identity and keeps known derivatives together; it cannot identify different encodings or excerpts of the same semantic source. A camera-authenticated independent corpus remains necessary for strong deployment claims.
2. **Development comparisons are exploratory.** Prompts, quality logic, model choice, frame sampling, and specialist thresholds have been evaluated on development results. These results are useful for selecting candidates and exposing regressions, not measuring final generalization. Freeze all choices before calibration, and use holdout once for final reporting.
3. **Small denominators are weak evidence.** Zero false positives among 12 real smoke videos has a nominal Wilson 95% upper bound of approximately 24.2%, not zero population FPR. With 36 real holdout originals, even zero observed false positives has a nominal Wilson upper bound of approximately 9.6%. These intervals assume independent original units; hash-derived grouping does not establish that condition or guarantee the stated population coverage. Three CGI excerpts are one known source family. Per-generator estimates often contain only five originals before splitting.
4. **Initial improvement is not statistically established.** In the first 35-clip comparison, AI hits improved on two clips and regressed on one. Exact paired two-sided McNemar p=1.0. The extra net hit and reduced confident negatives should be described as observed behavior in a small development comparison, not proof of greater underlying accuracy.
5. **Transformations are simulated.** H.264 recompression/cropping/resampling and a black bar do not reproduce every Instagram/TikTok/YouTube/X processing path. No actual social reupload was performed by this corpus builder. All transformed derivatives must remain with their source group; 690 rows are not 690 independent videos.
6. **Abstention cannot stand in for detection.** AI abstentions count as recall misses. Inspect AI recall, explicit false negatives, abstention, false positives, and completion together. Class metrics exclude API errors; incomplete scans must never be advertised as successful end-to-end accuracy. A changed wording or lower confidence alone does not count as a recovered detection.
7. **A corpus posterior is not a deployment posterior.** False-negative contamination among reported negatives depends on the artificial class prevalence and selection criteria. It is not the probability that an arbitrary Instagram negative result is wrong. LLM scores, SAFE logits, and D3 statistics remain uncalibrated ranking/evidence signals.
8. **Intervals need the right unit.** Row-level Wilson intervals assume independence. Use registered-group resampling for known derivatives, paired tests for before/after comparisons, and report small sample sizes. Existing “source-group bootstrap” results preserve only the registered relationships, not proven semantic-source independence; unrecognized families can still make intervals too narrow or invalidate nominal paired-test significance. Original-file outcomes must not be labeled independent-original outcomes. Grouped cross-validation blocks known derivative leakage, but cannot establish that undocumented capture or prompt/reference families do not cross folds. A percentile bootstrap degenerates with zero observed errors and does not prove a zero upper bound.
9. **Selection and cue shortcuts remain possible.** The deterministic sample uses a public benchmark distribution. The downloader has a 20 MB cap, but the cached pinned archive's largest MP4 is 3,203,007 bytes, so that cap excludes no video member in this revision. Existing benchmark curation/encoding, source watermarks, semantic content, and generator-specific visual styles may act as shortcuts. Results do not yet isolate detection of synthesis from recognition of a dataset or generator branding.

## Execution provenance

The runner validates clip hashes and split membership, isolates ground-truth labels from adapter inputs, snapshots detector/prompt source, and bundles executable local modules before calls. Saved runs include a source/configuration/bundle fingerprint; a changed detector cannot silently resume into an older experiment. Model-provider revisions and behavior remain outside that local fingerprint. When reporting existing predictions, retain the original run plan and output alongside any regenerated summary.

**Scan completion and model-pass completion are separate measures.** A scan can return a valid HTTP response and an inconclusive result after an inspection or adjudication times out. The runner currently counts that as a completed scan, not a top-level API error. Alongside scan errors, report attempted/completed/failed passes by pass name, scans containing any failed pass, and the failed-pass subset's AI recall and abstention. Read the saved result's `videoAnalysis.inspection.passes` statuses; distinguish a conditional adjudication that was not required from one that was attempted and failed. Preserve the original failure result when comparing retries. Do not say that all model calls worked merely because all scans returned results, or exclude degraded scans from the primary end-to-end denominator.

Do not count the fixture adapter as detector evidence. The BusterX authors designate GenBuster-Bench as evaluation, and their training scripts use separate datasets; this does not independently establish exact-clip or pretraining separation for the released checkpoint. See [BusterX assessment](BUSTERX_ASSESSMENT.md) before considering it as a candidate. The local D3 and SAFE experiments were rejected from production based on development performance.

SPAI offered complementary detections on original development clips but failed the default-threshold simulated repost test (3/23 mild, 0/23 severe). Its 35-clip exploratory OR fusion must not be used to support claims about Instagram robustness. Six controls also found only one of three mixed inserts; sparse sampling and averaging require separate validation for partial AI content.
