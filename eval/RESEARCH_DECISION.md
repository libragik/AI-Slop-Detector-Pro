# Research decision

**Detection accuracy is not solved. No further inference or threshold experiments on the already tested local candidates are justified by the present evidence.** The strongest next route is a licensed video-specific candidate evaluated on independently documented source media, including actual platform processing. No external service has yet passed that evaluation here.

The subsequent [receipt-backed generation diagnostic](./GENERATED_ORIGIN_CONTROL.md) strengthens the failure evidence beyond author-labeled benchmark clips: the frozen app missed both a directly generated eight-second Veo clip and a two-second insert from it. The matched conventional CGI control was negative. All six passes completed; both mixed reviews described the insert, so that miss is not solely a sampling omission. This is one generated source and no claim of population accuracy. Detector settings were unchanged and these failures are retained.

The local specialist checks repeatedly reuse 35 development sources and their derivatives. The CGI/mixed controls represent only two underlying scenarios. Both Corvi and native SPAI sampled zero inserted-source frames in the 0.5-second and one-second controls, and two in the two-second control; SPAI's exact decoded bytes were separately verified. Their negative outputs expose a sampling/whole-pipeline limitation, not isolated failure to recognize pixels that were never presented. The parent carries an author's whole-video label but lacks a generation receipt, generation-mode record or per-frame origin evidence. These controls do not prove an initial static reference-image hold either. See the [mixed-control qualification](./MIXED_CONTROL_PROVENANCE.md). These small counts are not population accuracy estimates. Higher AUROC, more abstentions, a successful download, or an ensemble of correlated opinions is insufficient evidence of improvement.

## Evidence that would justify the next experiment

1. **Independent origin records:** newly generated clips with generator/version, job receipts and original file hashes; original camera recordings with retained capture/source records; conventional CGI with documented production origin; and mixed edits with parent hashes and edit timelines. Include smooth/static AI footage, brief inserts, human/TTS narration and ordinary difficult real footage. A fresh generated control can prove one origin-specific failure or success; it cannot establish detector accuracy alone.
2. **Matched delivery conditions:** preserve each original and controlled derivatives, then obtain authorized actual platform-processed versions with URLs and byte hashes. Local compression simulations cannot establish Instagram round-trip behavior. Keep every version of a source in the same split and conceal origin metadata from inference.
3. **One preregistered candidate:** choose a service/model version with appropriate access, freeze preprocessing, sampling, thresholds and failure handling, then evaluate actual pixels. Require usable recall in every declared quality group, conservative false-positive accounting, and separate CGI/mixed gates before consuming a new untouched test. Preserve pass failures, abstentions, latency and cost alongside decisions.
4. **A genuinely new test source:** the 75-clip holdout is consumed. More files from GenBuster are only file-disjoint; without camera/creator/prompt provenance they do not establish independent origin. The untouched thirty calibration clips remain small and from that same archive; they must not be consumed to rescue a candidate already rejected by development controls.

Until a candidate passes, the product can report observed indicators and limitations, and distinguish trusted provenance/disclosure from inferred origin. It cannot responsibly promise that a negative result establishes camera authenticity or that an uncalibrated score is a probability.

After the trained candidate failed, a [bounded new-route check](./POST_DINOV2_ROUTE_DECISION.md) found NPR's residual approach scientifically distinct but without established compatible checkpoint permission or a verified video runtime. It remains deferred. The next concrete proposal is a [five-control Sightengine API diagnostic](./SIGHTENGINE_API_DIAGNOSTIC_PLAN.md), prepared locally with no remote inference. Suitable paid video API access is missing; a subscription decision belongs to the user. Even a pass would only justify a separate fresh-source evaluation, including actual platform processing.

A [shot-based AEGIS assessment](./AEGIS_SHOT_STUDY_ASSESSMENT.md) motivated a separate wrapper and a fully frozen 105-case design. Its actual [45-negative stage failed](./AEGIS_SHOT_DEVELOPMENT.md): improved temporal coverage did not prevent false flags on camera-origin footage and conventional CGI. The selected sources included three [agency camera archives](./CAMERA_CONTROL_INSPECTION.md) and two newly rendered CGI parents, with complete construction and source-frame receipts. The [local Wan route](./WAN_LOCAL_FEASIBILITY.md) and Veo generation helper were prepared and tested offline, but the failed negative gate stopped all four new generations and the 28.929 GB Wan weight download. Neither generation-helper tests nor unexecuted future slots establish detector accuracy. This result does not reopen either failed AEGIS candidate.

The initial decision record used existing audits, a read-only decoder-byte comparison, installed SDK inspection and a bounded official-documentation access check. Later updates record the separately preregistered one-generation/three-upload diagnostic, the three-control AEGIS screen, the fresh shot/window negative stage, and the completed DINOv2 training experiment. No threshold fitting, calibration reads or consumed-holdout reruns followed these failures. Both AEGIS candidates and the trained DINOv2 candidate ran successfully and failed their declared detection gates. Conditional fresh-source acquisition and generation for a passing candidate remain unexecuted. The accuracy goal remains unmet. The user confirmed there is no existing specialist-detector API account. No access agreement, account creation or external contact was performed.
