## Deterministic construction and exact cohort

Every evaluated master has the same five-second, silent, 120-frame timeline: 640×360 square pixels, 24 fps, H.264 `libx264`, CRF 20, preset `medium`, two encoder threads, `yuv420p`, `setsar=1`, stripped metadata, explicit `-r 24 -fps_mode cfr`. Freeze the complete encoding command and FFmpeg binary hash with the construction code. Preserve the original parents. Display-aspect-correct, aspect-preserving scale to fill the target rectangle, then center crop; do not add letterboxes. This may crop USGS overlays and is recorded. Do not optically interpolate, denoise, or invoke a generative enhancement model. Native source frames may be repeated or dropped by the fixed frame-rate conversion; record the exact source index selected for every master frame.

Construction strictly decodes native RGB with the separately frozen builder's OpenCV 4.12.0 runtime and FFprobe PTS. It also requests `setNumThreads(0)` on GCD and verifies a reported thread count of 1. For output frame `j`, select the source frame whose presentation interval contains `excerptOffset+(j+0.5)/24`, with a boundary selecting the later frame. This is a frame-interval rule, not nearest-frame-center selection. Use source PTS and positive frame durations, not nominal frame numbers when they disagree. Missing source coverage is a construction failure; never extend a short parent by repeating its final decoded frame. Record decoder and distribution versions, complete source indices, native RGB hashes and timestamps.

For native width `w`, height `h` and rational sample aspect ratio `r`, use `s=max(640/(w*r),360/h)`, resized width `2*ceil(w*r*s/2)` and resized height `2*ceil(h*s/2)`. Resize native RGB with `cv2.INTER_CUBIC` to those dimensions, then crop 640×360 from integer offsets `floor((resizedWidth-640)/2)` and `floor((resizedHeight-360)/2)`. This corrects display aspect with only the declared even-pixel rounding. Preserve all dimensions/coordinates in the receipt; no content-aware crop.

Encode each selected parent interval once as an intermediate CRF-20 canonical video, and retain its hash. Strictly decode those intermediates to construct each edit. **Every evaluated master, including the unedited identity controls, receives one additional identical CRF-20 encode.** Thus identity and edited masters have the same two encoding passes; the intermediate videos are not extra detector inputs. Mild/severe derivatives each add one further encode directly from the evaluated master. Record the pass lineage; do not compare a once-encoded identity with a twice-encoded edit without disclosure.

All edits use the host timeline unchanged outside the donor interval; the donor uses its own master interval **`[1.5,3.5)`**. The inserted interval is also **`[1.5,3.5)`**, exactly host indices **36 through 83**, inclusive, mapped to donor indices **36 through 83**. The equal host/donor playback coordinates are the frozen source-plan choice. This central location is deliberately fixed, not evidence of arbitrary-position detection.

- **Hard cut:** replace all pixels of those 48 host frames with donor frames 36 through 83. Host resumes at frame 84 on its original timeline. No inserted hold, hidden audio cue or transition.
- **Continuous-blended:** retain the continuously advancing host underneath, and blend the full-frame donor over the same interval. For local insertion frame `k=0..47`, use donor frame `36+k` and let `u=(k+0.5)/24`. Set alpha to `u/0.25` for `u<0.25`, to 1 for `0.25<=u<1.75`, and to `(2-u)/0.25` otherwise. Compute `numpy.rint((1-alpha)*host + alpha*donor)` in float64 independently for each 8-bit RGB channel, with nearest/ties-to-even rounding, clamped to `[0,255]`, then encode. Retain pre-encode frame hashes. This is arithmetic in encoded RGB values, not a linear-light optical composition. The first and last six donor frames ramp; the middle 36 frames are fully donor. This is a two-second blended temporal insertion with 1.5 seconds fully donor. It is **not guaranteed to be cut-free** under `scdet`, and does not test a small spatial insert or every form of continuous synthesis.

Use the same recipes for negative-only donors and generated donors. Do not adjust an edit to obtain an expected scene boundary or score. Inspect resulting files and compare frame/timeline receipts to the specification before inference. Source-origin label and interval metadata are used by the evaluator only; AEGIS receives only the decoded frame tensor.

## Shot detection and exact temporal sampling

Use the installed, frozen FFmpeg 7.0 build with `scdet=threshold=10:sc_pass=0`. Apply it to **every decoded native-resolution frame of each evaluated file**, before model resize and without a preliminary frame-rate conversion, crop, denoise or blur. The study inputs are fixed-CFR `yuv420p`; preserve that native pixel representation for scene detection. Retain the exact filter graph and binary/version hashes. `scdet` boundaries are image-change observations, not verified semantic cuts. [FFmpeg 7.0 implementation](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/n7.0/libavfilter/vf_scdet.c), [filter documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#scdet).

The implementation compares its internal score inclusively to the threshold, but prints the score rounded to three decimals. Therefore select boundaries using the **presence of `lavfi.scd.time` on that frame**, never by re-thresholding the rounded `lavfi.scd.score`. Join each metadata row to its decoded frame ordinal and exact integer PTS/time base. Do not recover boundaries by approximately parsing a rounded log timestamp. Retain all per-frame scene metadata, including non-boundaries.

Number decoded frames `0..N-1` in presentation order. Require one video stream, constant dimensions/pixel format, no rotation, finite positive rational fps `F`, valid strictly increasing PTS, and complete decode through the last frame. Verify CFR by comparing normalized PTS to `i/F` within one input time-base tick, and verify final frame duration against `1/F` within that tolerance. Unsupported VFR or ambiguous timestamps cause abstention; do not silently estimate a new frame rate. The exact playback endpoint is `N/F` under this verified contract. Store original PTS, normalized rational timestamps and ordinal-derived endpoints; decimal strings are display only.

The first frame starts the first shot. For every marked boundary at index `b>0`, the boundary frame **starts the new shot**: the previous shot is `[a,b)` in frame indices and `[a/F,b/F)` in playback time. Ignore a redundant first-frame marker; deduplicate repeated metadata for the same frame. The last shot ends at `N`, not at the timestamp of its last frame. Never discard a boundary frame, add it to both shots, shift a boundary toward an annotated AI interval, merge a short shot into a neighbor, or omit a difficult region.

For each shot of `L` frames:

1. It is supported only when `L/F >= 1` second, `L>=8` decoded frame positions, and the input shortest side is at least 128 pixels. Eight frame positions does not mean eight unique images: exact content duplicates are recorded, not relabeled as decode fabrication. A shot below these bounds is unsupported coverage, with its full interval retained.
2. Let `W=floor(4*F)` and `S=floor(2*F)`. Both must be positive and `W>=16`; the frozen 24/12-fps strata satisfy this. If `L<=W`, take one whole-shot window `[0,L)`.
3. Otherwise take local starts `0,S,2S,...` while start `<=L-W`, then add the final start `L-W` if not already present. Each window is `[start,start+W)`. Sort starts ascending. Tail alignment is mandatory and no window crosses the shot boundary. At the selected frame rates these are exact four-second windows at two-second strides, plus a possible shorter final stride.
4. In a window containing `Q` native frame positions, select the 16 indices `start + floor(k*(Q-1)/15)` for `k=0..15`, then add the shot's global starting index. This matches the official integer linspace behavior. For a supported short shot with 8–15 positions, repeated requested indices are intentional and recorded. No fallback repeats or zero frames may replace a requested frame that failed decoding.
5. Deduplicate identical start/end windows and identical 16-index vectors **within the same file only**, retaining all aliases in the sampling receipt. Identical pixel content at different indices is not grounds to suppress a window. Never deduplicate across different quality variants, masters or parent files.

Before model execution, verify every planned requested frame was decoded with the expected shape and that all shot/window intervals collectively cover the entire playback timeline. A covered interval means the prescribed sampling window was processed; it does **not** mean every frame was classified. Save requested/decoded source indices and rational times, normalized tensor hashes, input shape, detected boundaries, shot/window endpoints, decoded-content duplicates, and the exact correspondence to edit lineage. Do not use annotated donor boundaries to define the model windows.

## Video decision, errors and resource bounds

Process all declared valid windows of a video; do not stop at its first positive. A video is positive if **any valid window** is positive. If no window is positive and any interval is unsupported, a decode/model output fails, or coverage cannot be verified, its result is **abstain**, not negative. A negative result requires all prescribed coverage and every valid window below 0.5. An observed positive with an error elsewhere remains a positive with incomplete coverage; the **stage still fails** because processing/coverage errors never satisfy a gate. Nonfinite logits/scores are errors, never negatives. Report false flags per complete video and per parent/family, not per selected window.

Frozen operational limits per file: 50,000,000 bytes, longest side 2,048 pixels, at most 120 seconds, at most 30,000 native frames, at most 128 detected shots and **32 distinct inference windows**. Allow **120 seconds for the complete per-video operation**, including decode, scene metadata and every window, with an 8 GiB resident-memory bound for the detector worker. A 0.1-second resource watcher terminates a worker that exceeds a bound; this polling guard may observe a transient memory overshoot. These are maximum work bounds, not thresholds chosen for accuracy. Never keep only the first permitted windows when a limit is exceeded; record abstention/resource failure. Abort a crashed or memory-exceeding worker without a lower-quality retry. The frozen five-second selections should be far below the duration/frame/window bounds; short-shot eligibility is still enforced.

Preflight coverage errors remain part of the selected stage even when no model call is needed for that file. Continue the other declared files in the current stage unless a global runtime failure prevents it; record every unrun selected row and reason. A stopped or incomplete stage cannot pass. No resume may overwrite raw outputs or alter the frozen policy; an integrity-preserving technical continuation must retain the failed attempt and does not erase its gate failure.

## Frozen staged acquisition and rejection gates

The complete 105-file design, including future generator prompts/seeds and pairings, is fixed **before Stage 1**. Stages save generation work after a rejection; they do not remove the remaining objective. Freeze manifests with opaque IDs and verified file hashes before each stage's inference, derived mechanically from the original plan. Every manifest must preserve full ancestry and the complete stage denominator.

**Preparation, no detector calls.** Freeze the protocol/adapter/plans, prepare all five negative parents and 45 negative files, verify source and construction receipts, and pass offline tests of boundaries, tail alignment, short shots, missing frames, nonfinite outputs, resource-limit abstention, complete-stage membership and immutable gate validation. Source inspection may check technical validity and origin, but cannot select alternate content. The adapter sees only video tensors; labels and generator names remain in the reporting layer.

**Stage 1 — all 45 negatives.** Run every declared negative master/edit/quality file. Require **45 complete negative results, zero positive files, zero abstentions, zero unsupported intervals and zero processing/integrity errors**. Any false flag or failure rejects this wrapper and stops before any new Veo generation or full Wan acquisition. Record the unacquired/unrun positive portion explicitly. A successful negative stage must have unchanged output, source, recipe and weight hashes before later stages can consume its gate receipt.

**Stage 2 — both Veo parents and all 30 resulting files.** Only after Stage 1 passes, execute the two already selected Veo requests once, retain all outputs/receipts, build the exact 30 files and evaluate the full stage. Require **30 complete positives, zero misses, zero abstentions, zero unsupported intervals and zero processing/integrity errors**, including both edit types in both host categories and each quality stratum. A failure stops before full Wan acquisition and generation. It is not grounds to choose another provider, prompt, crop or insertion interval.

**Stage 3 — both Wan parents and all 30 resulting files.** Only after the unchanged earlier gates pass, acquire the fixed public Wan files, execute its separate frozen runtime contract and the two normal generation requests, then evaluate all 30 corresponding files. Require the same **30 complete positives and zero errors/abstentions/unsupported intervals**. A runtime, acquisition or generation failure leaves the study incomplete; it does not validate a Veo-only detector. Earlier negative and positive gates must still be valid under exactly the same detector recipe.

**Overall gate:** all **45 negative files correct and all 60 positive files detected**, with all prescribed coverage and zero failures, under one unchanged wrapper/weight/threshold recipe. A failure in any stratum rejects the candidate as configured for this application. No threshold, cut threshold, window size, stride, normalization, model head or clip selection changes after outputs. Any redesign is a separate development candidate requiring a new preregistered study and fresh source footage.

Even a complete pass authorizes only larger independent-source validation. It is too small and dependent to establish an acceptable population false-positive rate, sensitivity to arbitrary temporal positions, audio-origin detection, spatial compositing, ordinary human/social scenes or actual Instagram transport robustness.
