## Inference contract and concrete gaps

This method estimates image-diffusion scores and combines them with temporal differences, then compares a learned video representation against a real reference distribution using MMD. It therefore differs from the already tested frame classifiers and language-model judgments. The released [test configuration](https://github.com/ZSHsh98/NSG-VD/blob/9a60f57fcb464b418899110a4717c8c8fd7687bc/configs/nsg-vd-224x224/test.yaml) uses eight frames, 224-square processing, 100 reference videos, a learned 300-dimensional Swin representation, and a fixed score-above-one decision in [test_dMMD.py](https://github.com/ZSHsh98/NSG-VD/blob/9a60f57fcb464b418899110a4717c8c8fd7687bc/test_dMMD.py). A domain-matched reference bank is part of the detector, not optional evaluation bookkeeping. It must exclude all assessed source families and remain fixed across quality variants.

The [frame extractor](https://github.com/ZSHsh98/NSG-VD/blob/9a60f57fcb464b418899110a4717c8c8fd7687bc/data/video_frames_dataset.py) takes frames at integer interval `max(1,total_frames//8)` from frame zero, saves JPEGs, and caps output at eight. The [feature loader](https://github.com/ZSHsh98/NSG-VD/blob/9a60f57fcb464b418899110a4717c8c8fd7687bc/data/feature_dataset/score_feature_dataset.py) stretches them with Lanczos to 224-square, uses centered temporal differences internally and one-sided endpoint differences, and multiplies endpoint NSG features by 100 on loading. These choices must be preserved or explicitly declared as an adaptation. This sparse path does not guarantee coverage of brief inserts.

There is a reproducibility ambiguity before any port: the paper describes timestep `5/1000` and a 256-square diffusion model; the released default computes `(diffuse_steps+1)/1000 = 6/1000` with diffusion input resolution 224. The backbone's configured architecture is 256-square, which alone does not resolve the intended inference input. The released test/reference helpers contain unconditional `.cuda()` calls despite an initial CPU fallback, and the backbone config enables FP16. No official Mac CPU/MPS contract or latency measurement was found. The 2.4 TB README requirement describes its full training/dataset workflow, not a demonstrated minimum inference footprint. Dependencies are unpinned. Do not silently substitute resolution, precision, time, references, or checkpoint variant.

## Detection evidence and decision

The [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08073v1) evaluates held-out generator families and scraped synthetic video, but I found no explicit conventional-CGI-negative evaluation or controlled JPEG/H.264/repost robustness study. The authors acknowledge domain-shift and discontinuous-motion limitations. The [publisher's SEINE MMD-MP result file](https://github.com/ZSHsh98/NSG-VD/blob/9a60f57fcb464b418899110a4717c8c8fd7687bc/assets/nsg-vd-test_results/standard-SEINE-mmd-mp.csv) reports mean recall 98.54%, precision 82.63%, and AUROC 97.68%; these are their benchmark results, not ours, and do not establish our required low false-positive rate. Reference-domain coverage materially affects the method, so a new bank cannot be chosen after seeing test outcomes.

**Defer inference until the preprocessing ambiguity and fixed independent reference-bank design are resolved.** Its public access makes a later bounded evaluation possible; it does not justify immediate integration, a confidence claim, or reopening consumed holdout data. Commercial deployment has the additional dependency-license condition above. This audit establishes no candidate that has passed our accuracy requirements.

The parent's additional CVPR 2026 lead, [Your One-Stop Solution for AI-Generated Video Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Ma_Your_One-Stop_Solution_for_AI-Generated_Video_Detection_CVPR_2026_paper.html), identifies itself as AIGVDBench: a benchmark of existing detectors. Its title is not evidence of a new deployable checkpoint.
